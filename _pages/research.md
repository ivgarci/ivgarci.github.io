My research focuses on **Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) networks**, combining network science, graph algorithms and physical communication constraints.

## Main interests

- **Network design:** geographical clustering, trusted-relay placement and topology optimisation.
- **Robustness:** connectivity, critical nodes and links, community structure and resilience to failures.
- **Performance:** secret-key-rate-aware routing and sensitivity to optoelectronic parameters.
- **Infrastructure planning:** geographically realistic models and implications for EuroQCI.

## Doctoral research

My PhD at the **University of Salamanca (2026)** examined the generation and validation of large-scale QKD networks, using case studies in Castile and León, mainland Spain and railway-derived graphs.

These are **network models and design scenarios**, not claims of deployed QKD infrastructure.

## Open-source research

**[qkd-net-tools](https://github.com/ivgarci/qkd-net-tools)** provides the public Python and R code, datasets and analyses supporting the doctoral research.
